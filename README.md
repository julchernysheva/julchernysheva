### Research practice

I explore how technological systems reshape human agency, memory, authorship and the ways we understand reality. My work moves between research, editorial systems, interactive environments and artistic experiments.

**ART.IS.YOU** is my independent research platform on people, culture and technology.

### Selected research / Russian Intellect

**[Russian Intellect](https://artisyou.ru/research/russian-intellect/)** maps the continuities between Russian scientific schools, mathematical methods and contemporary technologies. Its interactive research map connects 81 people, 28 technologies and 8 companies.

[Explore Russian Intellect →](https://artisyou.ru/research/russian-intellect/)
